## Objective and scope

Qualify one additional real-process boundary using existing native APIs: an authorized Once decision has committed, its release task is approved Ready, the process dies before that intent reaches dispatch, the original grant expires, and a separately restarted Preparing host automatically continues that exact committed intent. Preserve a previously succeeded parallel sibling and the original approval audit/budget. The parent owns every process, socket and artifact.

RFC9.3 requires atomic consumption plus ready intent and surviving loss after commit; RFC9.5 says accepted intent does not expire retroactively; RFC11.4 step4 requires redispatch of committed ready intents before readiness. Existing native_crash.rs347–409 covers one accepted continuation with explicit fixture recovery and attempts_used0. It does not establish a real Preparing restart, a succeeded sibling, inspector epoch consumption, or readiness cuts. Existing startup_restart.rs has exactly2 credited parent lanes; their accepted group and seven sources remain exact. This is a separate target and one new parent, not a reclassification or third member of their two-lane contract.

Exclude production API/behavior/CLI/default changes, manifests/lock changes, old-test edits, original Node/TypeScript reruns, providers, persistentAlways, cancellation, incompatible/revoked-policy automatic blocking and global RFC11.4/core/M0/CP010/broad56 closure. Existing APIs may pass initially. A coverage extension needs no artificial behavioral RED; compile/setup/timing/abnormal cleanup failures never supply such credit.
