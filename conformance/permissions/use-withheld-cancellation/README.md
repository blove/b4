# Withdraw a permission-use callback withheld before entry

This profile covers explicit embedded cancellation using a returned, drained Driver failure that actually retains renewed NewUse Work after its local entry hook returned false. A Dispatch snapshot, public prepared invocation or absent callback status supplies no withdrawal authority. The actual Work is uninvoked and remains uniquely owned through current management checks and the storage write.

The operation derives both real Kernel transitions, CancelRequested4 and Cancelled5, and atomically persists their execution/thread changes and journals. It preserves original approval/decision/consumed grant, saved permission and Use evidence. A fresh later reuse can be checked after ordinary close/reopen. No native receipt or cancellation acknowledgement is fabricated.

The focused fixture uses genuine human Always acceptance, original Ready cancellation and subsequent NewUse authorization. Its controls cover exact double-transition projections and repeat refusal; current identity/catalog/target refusals and revoked-effect constraint with authorized management stop; exact late terminal-journal rollback followed by healthy retry of the same actual retained failure; and refusal to treat real entered uncertainty or a panicking admission hook as withheld authority. Full typed/kernel/nine-table captures and bounded normal owner retirement accompany the relevant observations.

A storage error alone leaves durable state unconfirmed. The exact injected precommit guard, full rollback, guard removal and healthy same-owner control establish the tested fault boundary. They do not imply rollback for arbitrary Store errors or safe automatic replay. Explicit retries retain actual Work and the same cumulative evidence account; they perform no callback invocation and must recheck exact source/fences.
