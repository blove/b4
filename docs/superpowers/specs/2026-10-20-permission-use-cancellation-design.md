## Receipt and terminal semantics

The callback already owns its actual renewed invocation before cancellation. The controlled call must remain under the existing worker/capacity lifetime and outside the state mutex. Preserve uncertainty, panic, invalid output and rejected outcome in actual owned receipts, with no replay. A real controlled Cancelled acknowledgement means callback-owned work/descendants have stopped; it becomes receipt-backed EffectOutcome::Cancelled only after the controlled native entry validates observer intent.

Extend the typed outcome path narrowly to validate the exact current CancelRequested4 and its additional cancellation journal against the original sealed NewUse claim, physical indexed immutable authority/Use bytes and unchanged invocation. An immediate transaction derives terminal revision5 from the actual retained outcome and current kernel snapshot: CancelAttempt for a genuine acknowledgement, Complete for an actual success, or FailAttempt for an actual known failure. Preserve the kernel's actual state and result/failure fields rather than substituting an acknowledgement or discarding owned evidence. Complete after a request retains the real success result with a cancelled execution; the current FailAttempt relation may produce Failed and must not be forced to Cancelled. An ordinary no-cancellation controlled success remains Complete4 through the existing writer relation.

Adapt the Driver completion/candidate tickets alongside the source writers: current CancelRequested4 must be the exact kernel Cancel projection of the same sealed Running3 invocation, and terminal5 must derive from that actual receipt. Recheck that relationship during immediate commit and same-Work retry. Cancellation request, source capture and terminal write must recheck exact current fences and histories. A stale target, changed owner/context/catalog/evidence, missing durable cancellation or signal-only acknowledgement refuses without manufacturing a terminal state. Do not allow arbitrary CancelRequested snapshots or revision numbers to serve as permission authority. The original creator history reader and old direct/original paths retain their existing accepted relations.

A late terminal storage failure leaves actual CancelRequested4 and the same actual receipt/outcome Work retained. A known pre-commit request fault, such as the exact journal abort, rolls back to Dispatch3. An unconfirmed/unknown store outcome is not proof of rollback or permission to replay; publish no cancellation signal merely from an error. No automatic retry, refund, permission revocation or broad lifecycle capability is introduced.
