# Proposed implementation plan: bounded persistent exact-tool Always

Status: accepted source plan, 2026-10-03. The 58 broad plan items remain unchecked; qualified increments do not close their remaining integration requirements. Use the [accepted design](../../../spec/runtime/persistent-always-design.md). Embedded Always acceptance, token-free PermissionUse and the first Dispatch claim have progressed through separate source and behavior reviews. The [first Dispatch qualification](../../../conformance/permissions/use-dispatch/README.md), adopted 2026-10-08, covers persistence, late journal rollback and the full workspace regression. The [owned NewUse renewal qualification](../../../conformance/permissions/use-renewal/README.md), adopted 2026-10-09, covers complete durable provenance, current Claimed gate, independent final resource reread, account controls and the full workspace regression for the selected local single-task path. The [owned callback qualification](../../../conformance/permissions/use-callback/README.md), adopted 2026-10-09, covers eight finite local cases, six pure controls, a separate private first-entry refusal and the full workspace regression. Human and legacy renewal, durable outcome settlement, Driver integration, recovery and the broad capability remain pending. The selected option is an opt-in parallel-capable native profile with truthful human batches and immutable exact permissions. The user's continuous local-development authorization applies after the relevant Source gates; this plan supplies no separate tool permission or execution credit.

Already-correct controls may pass without an invented failure. Each stage has its own reviewed implementation and actual GREEN; a broad first Always failure cannot cover later gates.

Protect old constructors, old unsupported behavior, KernelCommand/Snapshot version 1, Once/Deny models/validation, driver capacity/drain/ownership, CLI defaults/demo/storage, manifests/lockfile and old genuine tests/helpers. NativeApplication::new_with_history retains its rejection of approval tasks in historical registrations. NewPermissionService/port/catalog/constraint authority are proposals, not present APIs. Every nonapproval native call in the opted-in profile is also constrained. Always does not become Once; future uses never mint a human token/grant. New authority cannot be consumed by a legacy host or existing confinement audit.
